Magic Flask: Sort Puzzle — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 October 2026

Magic Flask: Sort Puzzle (“Magic Flask”, “the app”) is a liquid sort puzzle game for Android made by Kozo Technology. This policy explains what information the app and its advertising partner handle, and the choices you have.

Contact: kozo.technology@gmail.com

Information the app stores on your device

The app keeps your game progress and settings on your device only:

  • the level you reached, the stars you earned and your game mode (Relaxed, Classic or Expert)
  • hints, spare flasks and gems, and the themes, liquid colours and glassware you unlocked
  • daily-potion streaks and opened milestone chests
  • sound, music, vibration and colour-symbol settings

The app has no accounts and does not send this information to us or anyone else. Uninstalling the app, or using Settings → Reset progress, deletes it. Gems are earned by playing and cannot be bought with money; the app has no in-app purchases.

Advertising and your consent

This app shows ads served by Google AdMob: optional rewarded videos that you choose to watch for a reward, and an occasional full-screen ad between games (never during your first levels). AdMob may collect your device's advertising ID, IP address (used for approximate location), device and operating-system information, and ad interaction data to serve and measure ads, and to prevent fraud. See how Google uses this information: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

  • European Economic Area, UK and Switzerland: on first launch the app asks for your consent through Google's certified consent management platform. Personalised ads are shown only if you consent; otherwise you may see limited, non-personalised ads, or no ads.
  • United States: if you live in a US state with a consumer privacy law (such as California), you can opt out of the sale or sharing of your personal information for targeted advertising.
  • Change your choice at any time: open the app's Settings → Privacy options. You can also reset your advertising ID or opt out of ads personalisation in your device's Google settings.

The game stays fully playable whatever you choose: undo and rewinding are always free, and hints and spare flasks can also be bought with gems earned by playing.

Permissions

The app uses internet access only to load ads and the consent form. The other permissions listed on Google Play (network state, advertising ID, keeping the device awake, background work) are added by Google's ads SDK. The app does not ask for your location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos or files.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, contact us and we will delete it.
